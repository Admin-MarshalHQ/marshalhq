# Setru privacy notice: publication gates

The review copy of `/privacy` is not a complete notice and is **not ready to publish**. The existing production notice remains unchanged; that is not legal clearance for existing intake. The legal-readiness review recommended holding new waitlist entries while these items are open. This draft does not alter public intake or pilot access. In local dev or an access-controlled Vercel preview only, set `SETRU_PRIVACY_REVIEW=1` to see the copy. Production cannot enable this draft through that flag.

Before replacing the published notice, obtain and document:

- **Operator and controller:** approval to publish the confirmed operator/controller legal identity, geographic establishment address, and applicable public register and number, VAT details, any authorisation or professional disclosures, and ICO registration/contact details if applicable. Do not use a proposed entity, private address or internal identifiers without confirmation. Review the applicability of the provider-information requirements with the adviser.
- **Contact:** choose the public inbox and verify receipt, replies, handling owner and cover. Routing of the Setru aliases to an existing mailbox was reported configured, not verified for delivery, replies or monitoring. Keep the founder login and destination inbox private unless separately approved for publication.
- **Purposes and lawful bases:** confirm a basis per activity and the legitimate interests where relied on. Resolve the waitlist invitation/update basis and withdrawal handling, including existing records; keep the receipt and one role update distinct from general marketing. Confirm the parties’ roles once booking contact is released.
- **Retention:** document periods or criteria for each dataset, including rejected applications and completed booking contacts; verify deletion, exceptions and backups. Do not promise a retention period, erasure outcome, or contact expiry without evidence.
- **Provider safeguards:** confirm account ownership, relevant hosting/processing regions, contracts, international transfers and safeguards, plus the scope of cookies and analytics in production.
- **Collection and exercise of rights:** resolve Article 13/14 applicability and notices for any historic or separately sourced records; confirm how requests are handled. Recheck the notice links and summary in the footer, shared waitlist form and support (already linked in the current build), and add any remaining collection notices needed for the authorised launch scope.

Primary sources: [ICO right to be informed](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/) and [Electronic Commerce Regulations 2002, regulation 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2013/regulation/6). Verify the final controller and service-provider statements with the founder/adviser before enabling collection or release.
