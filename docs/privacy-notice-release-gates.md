# Setru privacy notice: publication gates

The public pages describe development use, existing records, the synthetic demo and Support. The prospective intake notice remains a preview-only draft. New waitlist entries and registrations are held at the page and server action before validation, persistence or mail. Existing accounts, recovery, rights requests and the demo remain accessible. This change neither deletes records nor authorises real hiring.

For local fixture tests only, SETRU_FIXTURE_INTAKE=1 exercises the original intake flows. Hosted previews and production builds refuse this switch. Use SETRU_PRIVACY_REVIEW=1 in local development or a protected preview to inspect the prospective notice. Review and approve each intake route separately before reopening it.

Before replacing the published notice, obtain and document:

- **Operator and controller:** approval to publish the confirmed operator/controller legal identity, geographic establishment address, and applicable public register and number, VAT details, any authorisation or professional disclosures, and ICO registration/contact details if applicable. Do not use a proposed entity, private address or internal identifiers without confirmation. Record the establishment and applicable register, VAT and fee facts in the restricted decision paper.
- **Contact:** choose the public inbox and verify receipt, replies, handling owner and cover. Routing of the Setru aliases to an existing mailbox was reported configured, not verified for delivery, replies or monitoring. Keep the founder login and destination inbox private unless separately approved for publication.
- **Purposes and lawful bases:** confirm a basis per activity and the legitimate interests where relied on. Resolve the waitlist invitation/update basis and withdrawal handling, including existing records; keep the receipt and one role update distinct from general marketing. Confirm the parties’ roles once booking contact is released.
- **Retention:** document periods or criteria for each dataset, including rejected applications and completed booking contacts; verify deletion, exceptions and backups. Do not promise a retention period, erasure outcome, or contact expiry without evidence.
- **Provider safeguards:** confirm account ownership, relevant hosting/processing regions, contracts, international transfers and safeguards, plus the scope of cookies and analytics in production.
- **Collection and exercise of rights:** resolve Article 13/14 applicability and notices for any historic or separately sourced records; confirm how requests are handled. Recheck the notice links and summary in the footer, shared waitlist form and support (already linked in the current build), and add any remaining collection notices needed for the authorised launch scope.

Primary sources: [ICO right to be informed](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/) and [Electronic Commerce Regulations 2002, regulation 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/2013/regulation/6). Source reconciliation: `app/actions/waitlist.ts`, `app/actions/recovery.ts`, `app/actions/hiring.ts`, `app/actions/shifts.ts`, accepted booking pages, `lib/mail.ts`, and the amended legal-readiness pack. ## Operating a cleared private invitation

The public signup and waitlist server actions remain held. The private route is `/pilot/join`. Before deployment apply the additive `prisma/private-pilot-admission.sql` to the verified target and check drift. Existing users get no acceptance by default. Rolling back the app preserves both tables and all issued receipts.

Use the actual approved operator address, contact, processor and retention evidence in the restricted pack. Configure `SETRU_PILOT_DISCLOSURES` as a server-only JSON object with string fields `businessAddress`, `contactEmail`, `providersAndTransfers` and `retention`. The provider paragraph must identify relevant recipients, locations, applicable safeguards and how to request a copy. Include the applicable retention periods and backup limits. Enter only details authorised for publication to participants. Confirm the rendered documents in an isolated preview first. The terms and notice are based on documents 1–6 in the restricted participant pack. Each content change produces its own new document version; a prior receipt remains downloadable unchanged.

After the vacancy, mailbox, rota and participant-specific release evidence has cleared, set the exact addresses on the explicit role lists `PILOT_MANAGER_EMAILS` and `PILOT_MARSHAL_EMAILS`. The legacy untyped participant list cannot grant admission. Set `SETRU_PRIVATE_PILOT_COVER_UNTIL` to the actual end of the staffed period in UTC ISO format (ending in Z). New admission and code use close at that time. Enable `SETRU_PRIVATE_PILOT_ADMISSION=1` only for the cleared stage and share the route through the agreed channel. Each invited person requests a one-use 30-minute code delivered to their own inbox. It proves control of that inbox. It supplies no other verification. Signed-in existing participants use `/pilot/agreement`. Both groups must affirm age and terms and acknowledge receipt of the privacy notice separately. Setru saves the exact text, role, version and time, queues a copy to their inbox, and provides an authenticated downloadable copy in Settings. Check real receipt using designated accounts before inviting participants.

Remove the admission switch and redeploy to stop new registrations and acknowledgements. Existing account, booking-history, withdrawal, dispute and support routes remain. Pause the vacancy separately to stop further hiring where circumstances require it. Continue serving existing document copies. A database record or accepted email alone does not establish delivery. Public hiring requires its separate opening decision.

### PostHog measurement scope

Configure the server-only environment variables `POSTHOG_PROJECT_TOKEN` and `POSTHOG_HOST` together. Demo application and booking counts require the visitor to tick the demo checkbox. The waitlist count runs only for newly created entries. Use event totals filtered by environment. Each event has its own identifier, so unique-user and conversion-funnel reports would be misleading. No analytics runs on private hiring, attendance or support screens. A synthetic receipt verifies transport only.
